Signal not Noise · the stage's colour

Four grounds for the same scene.

The same two screens in each candidate: the stamped notice from beat 3.2, which carries every element (needle, card, stamps, verdict, the app wordmark), and the black hold that ends each movement. Verdict colours, judge colours and type stay fixed across all four; only the ground, the ink and the one accent change. Pick with the room in mind, not the laptop.

One fact about projectors shapes this: ambient light lifts a dark ground toward grey and flattens its contrast, while a light ground loses almost nothing. Conference rooms are rarely dark. A saturated ground is the riskiest: cheap projectors band and shift it.

My call: B, the noticeboard, with C's cards.

Light ground for the room, warm because the jokes are warm, cards white-ish so the stamps have an edge to sit on, and the burnt-orange accent only on the needle and captions. The thing that makes it not another cream deck is the cut to black on every hold: paper, paper, paper, black, and the thesis line. If you want more edge, D's hold (yellow line on black) can be the title card and the close only, with B everywhere else.
